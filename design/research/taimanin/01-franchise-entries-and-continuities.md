## 1. What the franchise is

*Taimanin* (対魔忍, "Anti-Demon Ninja") is a long-running adult franchise from the
Japanese studio **Lilith** (brands Lilith / Black Lilith; published by Infini-Brain,
later EXNOA and Shiravune for localisations). Creator and lead writer: **Sasayama
Ittousai**. Producer: EDEN. Principal artists: **Kagami Hirotaka** (Asagi line) and
**Aoi Nagisa** (Yukikaze line), with many guest artists in the online games.

It began in 2005 as a dark eroge visual novel and grew into visual novels, OVAs, live
action films, light novels, manga, drama CDs, a browser card game, a browser RPG, an
all-ages 3D action game and, in 2026, an all-ages turn-based squad RPG. Every entry is
treated here as a source on equal footing.

**Standard opening narration** (used by most entries, paraphrased): in near-future
Japan an ancient rule of non-interference between humans and demons has collapsed as
humanity slid into decadence; criminal syndicates of humans and demons operate in the
dark, and the government has organised ninja who can fight demons — the Taimanin.

**Tone by entry type**

| Entry type | Tone | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Mainline visual novels | Grim, near-dystopian; heroines are captured and a true ending must be earned against many bad endings | Adult, explicit, frequently dark (capture, coercion, body modification, mind control) |
| OVAs / live action / drama CDs | Mostly adapt the *bad-ending* routes; treated as "what if" branches | Adult |
| *Battle Arena*, *RPG/RPGX* | Lighter, serialised, large cast, comedy events next to serious arcs | Story events are safe-for-work; adult scenes are separate unlockable "what if" scenes (RPGX) |
| *Action Taimanin*, *GOGO!*, *Squad* | All-ages; action or comedy | No explicit content |

### 2.5 Shared-universe titles from the same studio

Lilith treats several unrelated-looking titles as part of the same world. The online
games retroactively pulled these into the Taimanin continuity:

*Witch of Steel Annerose* (Amidahara; witch-swordswoman detective), *Onmyo Knight
Towako* (Kyoto onmyoji), *Cara the Blood Lord* (vampire queen, Edwin Black's sister),
*Special Agent Rei & Fuko* (Section Four agents), *Shinigami* miniseries, *My Elf Elder
Sister*, *Fallen Wife* (set in a *Yukikaze 1* bad-ending branch), *Tentacle and
Witches*, and the far-future *Prison Battleship* series (about 200 years later, space
setting; characters cross over as dimension travellers).

## 3. The multiverse model (in-fiction)

The franchise explains its own contradictions with a cosmology. This is directly
useful for a "Gates between worlds" design.

- **Mother Sea / Mother Ocean** — a higher-dimensional void from which every universe is
  born. Universes drift, approach and sometimes interact.
- **Each Taimanin universe is a pair**: a **Human Realm** and a **Demon Realm** (Dark
  World, *Makai*), joined by **Demon Gates**.
- **Reset / Singularity** — a universe can be destroyed and reborn. A reset creates not
  one but a near-infinite spray of similar new universes. Edwin Black is the main known
  cause.
- Lilith's stated position: *everything is canon somewhere* — true endings, bad endings,
  "what if" scenes, OVAs — but not all in the same universe.
- **Brain Flayers** (an extra-dimensional species) index universes with a numbering
  scheme and are the usual in-story cause of characters being displaced between worlds.

### 3.1 The named continuities

| Tag | Official / wiki name | Contains | How it came to be |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[VN]` | **Old World** ("canon universe") | All mainline VNs except ZERO, plus the shared-universe titles | The original. Runs 2072–2082. |
| `[ZERO]` | **New World** | *Taimanin Asagi ZERO* | Born from the *Asagi 3* **Asagi route**, where Edwin Black wins, fuses with Asagi ("Vampire Asagi") and resets the world. Characters are younger and histories are scrambled. |
| `[TABA]` | Another World — Battle Arena | *Battle Arena* | Identical to `[VN]` up to 2082, but *Asagi 3* never happens; Kotaro Fuuma's rise delays Black's plan by four to five years. Ends with its own reset in 2086. |
| `[RPGX]` | Another World — RPG | *Taimanin RPG / RPGX* | Born from the TABA reset. Past is altered (see timeline doc). Starts 2082, has advanced to about 2088. |
| `[AT]` | Another World — Action | *Action Taimanin* | Close cousin of `[RPGX]` with its own differences. Starts 2082. |
| `[GOGO]` | Another World — GOGO | *Taimanin GOGO!* | Comedy world where humans and demons signed a peace treaty and Nomad dissolved. |
| `[SQ]` | Another World — Squad | *Taimanin Squad* | Mixes casts from TABA, RPGX and Action; own continuity. |

Other branches that appear on screen:

- **Bad-ending worlds** — each failed route of a VN is a real, separate universe.
- **ZERO-like worlds** — younger versions of Sakura, Murasaki and others are pulled from
  these into `[TABA]` and `[RPGX]`.
- **Post-Apocalyptic / "Dark Future" world** `[RPGX]` — a timeline where the Brain Flayer
  Conquerors Monarchy has overrun Earth; a Resistance of humans, demons and Taimanin
  fights on. Future versions of Yukikaze, Rinko, Asuka and others come from here.
- **Joke worlds** — e.g. a magical-girl school parody (April Fools material).
- **Crossover worlds** — other Lilith games' universes, visited via Brain Flayer portals.

### 3.2 The two endings of *Taimanin Asagi 3* (the hinge of the whole franchise)

| Route | Outcome | Leads to |
|---|---|---|
| **Asuka route** (harder to unlock; "golden" ending) | Asagi and her adopted daughter Asuka kill Edwin Black before the reset completes. Sakura, Murasaki and Kosuke survive. | The Old World continues. No game is set after it yet; a *Yukikaze 3* in this world has been mentioned in interviews. |
| **Asagi route** ("Vampire Asagi") | Asagi over-uses her demon form, loses to Black; he fuses with her and resets the world. | `[ZERO]`, and by extension the framework that the online universes sit in. |

## 4. Conflicts and uncertainty

- **Dates.** *GOGO!* launch is 2023-05-16 on Wikipedia but 2023-06-16 on the fan wiki.
  *Squad* is 2026-03-04 (Wikipedia) vs 2026-03-05 (wiki) — probably a time-zone
  difference. *Chaos Arena* has two dates because it was sold at an event first.
- **In-universe year of *Asagi 2*.** Originally one year after *Asagi 1* (2073); later
  retconned by *Asagi 3* and writer confirmation to **2076**. This shifts *Murasaki*
  and *Summer Days* (treated here as 2075).
- **"Canon" is contested.** The wiki uses "canon universe" only as shorthand for the
  original VN timeline. Online games freely retcon it (for example, when Gosha Academy
  was built, how Danjo Fuuma died, who destroyed the Koukawa clan).
- **Ages.** Entries disagree about student ages (see the Gosha document). *GOGO!* states
  that all students are over 20; older entries imply academy grades spanning early
  teens to university age.
- **What I could not check.** Gameplay-system wikis for the online games were not
  reachable (see README), so mechanics for those games are summarised from search
  snippets and marked as unverified in the JRPG structures document.
