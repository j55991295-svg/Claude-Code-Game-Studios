# 11 — Items and Equipment

> Part of the Taimanin research reference. See [README](README.md) for sources and
> continuity tags. Machines that fight on their own (drones, exoskeleton units,
> bioweapons) are treated as opponents in
> [10 — Enemies and Bestiary](10-enemies-bestiary.md).

## 1. Overview — four gear traditions

| Tradition | Made by | Runs on | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Taimanin gear** | Gosha's research teams and tailors, Section Three, traditional smiths | Taima particles | Personal, custom-made, built around one user's art |
| **Human military tech** | UFS (machines), Chinese Union (biology), Japan | Power cells, drugs, stolen demon tech | Mass-produced, standardised, anyone can use it |
| **Demon-realm magical tools** (魔具) | Demon smiths, witches, magicians | Mana | Rare, powerful, often cursed or alive |
| **Hell technology** | Demonologists, crime syndicates, black labs | Both | Hybrid organic-mechanical devices, mostly for control and modification |

A fifth, out-of-scale tier — **Brain Flayer relics** — sits above all of them.

## 2. Taimanin equipment

### 2.1 Weapons

Every trainee starts on the **ninjatō** (short straight sword). After awakening,
each ninja moves to a personal weapon chosen to amplify the art or tame its side
effects. Kunai and shuriken stay as universal sidearms — the five-pointed
shuriken is the emblem that gives Gosha ("five wheels") its name.

| Class | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Short blades | Ninjatō, kodachi, sai, katar | Baseline; everyone is at least competent. |
| Long blades | Katana, ōdachi, zanbatō (anti-cavalry blade) | Favoured by single-sword stylists. |
| Polearms | Naginata, spear, bō and other staves | Reach; staves for non-lethal work. |
| Heavy | Axes, great blades | Too heavy for non-Taimanin to lift. |
| Fist and leg | Gauntlets, greaves, claws | Particle-charged strikes. Claws are unpopular — hard to handle and associated with the traitor Oboro. |
| Thrown | Kunai, shuriken, blowgun darts, floating spheres steered by particles | Secondary and distraction weapons. |
| Flexible | Whips, chains, *kusarigama*, *kyoketsu-shoge*, wire | Binding and control. |
| Firearms | Pistols, sniper rifles, cannons | Looked down on: ordinary bullets do little to strong demons. Accepted when the user can charge the rounds. |
| Trick weapons | Weapon umbrellas, scroll-bullets preloaded with elemental arts | One-off specialist tools. |
| Familiars | Small demons, mechanical dolls, golems | Fight alongside or shoot for the user. |
| Weapon prostheses | Blade-deploying arms and legs | See §3. |
| **Limiters** | Twin pistols, a bazooka, and other custom shapes | Regulators for arts too strong to control — see [07](07-powers-and-ninja-arts.md) §2.6. A lesser grade only dampens feedback. |

**Named weapons** (a sample):

| Weapon | Owner | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Akkirokumon** (悪鬼六文) | Asagi Igawa | Ninja sword |
| **Ishikiri Kanemitsu** (石切兼光) | Rinko Akiyama | Katana, kept by a traditional smith |
| **Yogiri** ("Night Fog") | Tatsuro Akiyama | Blade |
| **Kōma** and **Shirakami** | Kurenai Shinganji | Paired blades |
| **Inosuke** | Gaiza Nisha | Zanbatō |
| **Juuroku Bashu** | Ryu Shinganji | Ōdachi |
| **Onikiri** ("Oni-cutter") | Chizu Mikami | Blade |

Makers: the ancient smith **Senketsu**; in the present, **Mitsuyo Tenta** (a Metal
Art user) and the workshops of Gosha and Section Three.

### 2.2 The Taimanin suit (対魔忍スーツ)

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Protection | Bulletproof, light, unrestrictive. |
| Particle-conductive | Lets the wearer charge the suit; partly driven by "ancient ninja arts" as well as modern materials. |
| Instant deployment | Can be put on in about a second — the setting's equivalent of a transformation sequence. |
| Custom-made | No standard issue. Tailors (for example Gahei Uchigen) build each to the wearer's art and taste; rebuilt after damage or when abilities change. |
| Why they look as they do | In-universe: arts that give off heat, cold or current, or need air contact, would destroy fuller coverage. |
| Typical features | Mouth-covering mask, mesh at hands and legs, light built-in electronics. |
| Lineage designs | Juniors often model theirs on a mentor's or parent's suit. |
| For non-Taimanin | Works as armour, without the particle benefits. |
| Knock-offs | Inferior copies circulate in the underworld, some as costume. |

Variants: the Demon-Realm Ryoumanin wear heavier, more mechanical **Dark Armor**.
Asagi has a white special-combat suit besides her usual purple one.

### 2.3 Field kit and facilities

Communicator; antidote kit; wingsuit; face mask; **cloaking** (optical camouflage —
by reflective material, screens or an art; also hides Gosha's entrances and
drop-ships); **Taima automatons** (particle-powered wooden training puppets with
wooden fists and water cannons); **holographic training rooms** (solid-light
environments and enemies with difficulty settings, built from light mind-reading,
holograms and robots — and capable of injuring trainees); **psycho-dive** rigs for
reading a sleeping captive's memories from their own point of view.

## 3. Human military and medical technology

| Item | Maker | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Prosthesis** (義体) | UFS best; Japan cruder | Civil models look and feel natural and return sensation; combat models add strength and hidden blades. Losing a limb can leave a ninja *stronger*. Anyone with one counts as a cyborg. |
| **Powered exoskeletons** | UFS, Japan | Infantry armour meant to match tanks and helicopters. UFS line: **XPS-11A Bone** (old, bulky, reliable, arm cannon and gatling) → **Alexander** (mainstay, all-climate, long-range) → **Claudius** (newest; one beats two Bones; energy rifle and blades). Japan: **PEX-88 Raiden** and **PEX-00 Shusui**, secretly boosted with demon-realm tech. |
| **Firearms** | Everyone | Handguns survive as cheap or prestige arms; militaries field rapid-fire weapons, hand-held railguns and **plasma rifles** (standard UFS issue). Weak against real demons unless very advanced. |
| **Gravity control tools** | UFS, Japan | Experimental push/pull, weight change and, at the limit, tiny black holes. High-maintenance prototypes, but effective even against Otherworld spirits. |
| **Nanomachines** | A mercenary developer; UFS | Injected or woven into suits for strength or drug-like effects. They attack demon cells — and when both are in one body the war between them grants power while slowly killing the host. |
| **Particle detectors** | UFS | Spot Taimanin at range. |
| **Living suit** | DSO; a Fuuma researcher | An organic "suit" that boosts strength and hardening, with a particle-strengthening device sewn in. Vulnerable to mental attack, liable to malfunction — and usable as a trap that paralyses its wearer. |

## 4. Drugs and consumables

**"Future drugs"** are products of demon-realm ingredients mixed with advanced
pharmacology. Taimanin stop the illegal trade and use the legal side themselves.

| Family | Use | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Stimulants and enhancers | Stamina, strength, reflexes | Addiction, burnout |
| Medication and suppressants | Managing the after-effects of capture and modification | Dependence |
| Antidotes | Standard kit against toxins and aphrodisiacs | — |
| Aphrodisiacs | Gas, liquid, pill and oil forms; the underworld's main coercion tool. Some are natural demon secretions. | Addiction, loss of judgement |
| Demonizing agents | Force partial or full demonization | Irreversible |
| Muscle-suppressants | Dosed to prisoners in transit | — |
| **Viruses** | **CD ("Creeping Death") virus** — airborne, turns humans into mutant creatures within seconds (UFS-made, stolen) `[AT]`; **P-virus** — a Brain Flayer weapon spread by bite or scratch `[RPGX]` | Outbreak |

## 5. Demon-realm magical tools

| Item | Function | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Hell Blade / magic sword** (魔剣) | Signature arm of Hell Knights and sword-bearing nobles. Forged by demon smiths from rare ores; carries an element or effect — fire, poison, wind, life drain. None has ever been seen to break. Examples: Ingrid's **Dark Flame**; Lina's **Storm Fang** and **Sakura Blossom**; Annerose's shape-changing, evil-eating **Vajrayaksa**. | The strongest are half alive; at least one became a creature seeking new masters and more blood. Mass-produced blades exist without strong magic. |
| **Orichalcum** | Rare demon-realm metal for magic weapons: conductive, and hard enough to withstand noble-level power. One mining family's control of it buys standing equal to the Nine Nobles. | Scarce; forgeries are common. |
| **Magical catalyst** (触媒) | Staffs, bracelets, incense burners that enable or amplify casting. | One too strong overwhelms a weak user. |
| **Grimoire** | Spellbooks that store spells for the unskilled; some grant wishes, lay curses or hold pocket worlds. | Often booby-trapped (explode, summon, teleport). Contraband in the human world. |
| **Sealing stone / summon stone** (封印石) | A plain rock holding an elemental spirit that can be released to fight. Gives strong magic to those with none — prized even by the poor. | Expensive; needs talent; the spirit is primal and hard to control. |
| **Magic crystal** | Crystallised mana, absorbable as nourishment. | Also used by some vampires as a torture implement. |
| **Noh mask** | Suppresses the wearer's emotions — fear, rage, shyness. Used by oni and a few Taimanin. | — |
| **Succubus ring** | Lets a magic-capable wearer enter and control dreams. | Cannot induce sleep; any true dream demon in the dream overrides it and burns the ring out. |
| **Contract rings** (誓約の指輪) | Bind the wearer to obey, protect or feel devotion toward the ring's owner, overriding will. | Several rings mean conflicting masters. |
| **Great River of the Underworld** | A crimson jewel given to the incubus lord by the Nine Nobles; allows dream invasion across realms; a badge of rank. | Unique. |
| **Mirror of Pure Glass** | Words spoken before it become "real" for the speaker. | Lies rebound. |
| **Mirror of the Lost** | Traps people in a pocket dimension and fights them with illusions of people they know. | Fought as a boss `[RPGX]`. |
| **Jötunheimr** | A self-replicating frost-phantom device that makes snow fall and changes a region's climate. | Grows stronger the longer it runs; only the parent unit breeds. |
| **Philosopher's Stone** (賢者の石) | Near-limitless magical power. Can force open an **Otherworld Gate**, or be implanted to force a Taimanin's Inner Demon. | Left alone it spawns homunculi. Once said to appear every hundred or thousand years; now turning up more often. |
| **Residue of Darkness** (闇の残滓) | A fragment left by a world Reset. Eaten by a suitable being, it makes a **Vessel of the Void** with power beyond most lords. | Full use requires surrendering the self to the entity in the fragment. |

## 6. Control and restraint devices

The franchise's capture plots rest on a consistent toolkit. Described here by
function.

| Device | Type | What it does | Removal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inhibitor shackles** | Demon-realm restraints | Hold "the strength of three ogres"; remote-controlled; a gag that doses a muscle-weakening drug hourly; switchable blindfold. | Not by force. |
| **Sealing piercings / magical piercings** | Magic | Block ability use, mark ownership, or induce arousal; a lighter form of a crest. Used on prisoners in Amidahara. | By the applier. |
| **Crests and slave contracts** | Magic | Bind the bearer to terms; may affect the mind. See [07](07-powers-and-ninja-arts.md) §5. | By the holder or on expiry. |
| **EVE chip** | Brain-stem implant (Washizu Group) | Slow, subliminal rewriting of values over weeks, using the victim's own rationalisations; none of the tell-tale signs of crude brainwashing. | Surgery. |
| **Dark Seed** (魔種) | Organic brain implant (Nomad) | Makes the designated master nearly impossible to oppose. A remote triggers pain, puppet-like unconsciousness, compelled affection, arousal, or death by petrification. | Only by a top demonologist — it grows into the brain. |
| **Brainwashing machine** | Hell-tech pod | Holds a subject for days: false memories, lowered resistance, simulated scenarios. Works even on the strong-willed. | — |
| **Hypno app** | Phone software | Light hypnotic conditioning; far weaker than the above `[RPGX]`. | — |
| **Explosive rings** ("amputation shackles") | Chinese Union | Remote limb-severing charges. Fakes are used for intimidation. | Key or disarm. |
| **Heretical acupuncture** | Demonic technique | Needles that write magical signals into nerves and soul over 3–5 days, cross-wiring pain and pleasure. | — |

## 8. Conflicts and uncertainty

- The lore wiki describes gear narratively; **none of the games' numeric item
  systems are documented there.** What is known of weapon and supporter gacha
  mechanics is collected, with caveats, in
  [12 — JRPG Structures](12-jrpg-structures.md).
- The Dark Seed's button effects differ between `[ZERO]` and `[TABA]`.
- Whether named swords have special properties is usually unstated; most are
  simply well-made blades made famous by their owners.

## 9. Design notes — structures worth borrowing

1. **Starter weapon → personal weapon at awakening.** A built-in mid-game moment
   where the character's weapon class is chosen to match their power.
2. **The suit as a single, upgradeable, character-specific armour slot** rather than
   many armour pieces; rebuilt when the character changes.
3. **Four crafting traditions with different currencies and risks** (particles /
   industry / mana / hybrid) map directly onto four gear trees.
4. **Powerful items that bite back**: living swords, booby-trapped grimoires,
   primal summon stones, catalysts that overwhelm weak users.
5. **Gear that helps the weak more than the strong** — sealing stones, ofuda,
   spell-loaded scrolls — gives non-gifted characters a path.
6. **Restraints as a mechanical layer**: items that seal skills, sap stats over
   time, or impose a status that only a specialist can remove.
7. **Prosthetics as a consolation mechanic**: a serious defeat converts into a new
   equipment slot.
8. **A top tier of unique relics** tied to world travel — natural quest objects for
   a game built around Gates.
