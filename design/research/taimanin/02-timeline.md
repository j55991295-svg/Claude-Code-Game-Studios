# 02 — Timeline

> Part of the Taimanin research reference. See [README](README.md) for sources and
> continuity tags. Date confidence follows the fan wiki's own convention:
> **solid** = a year is stated in a game or by the writer; **approx.** = derived from
> "about ten years ago" style lines; **order only** = placement is a guess and only
> the sequence should be trusted.

The mainline story runs **2072 → 2082** (ten to eleven years between *Asagi 1* and
*Asagi 3*). The online games start in 2082 and move forward roughly one in-game year
per real anniversary.

## 1. Deep history (shared by every continuity)

| Era | What happens |
|---|---|
| Primeval age | The Human World and the Dark World (Demon Realm) form out of the Mother Sea. |
| Pre-ancient | The **Aion**, a precursor species, visit the Demon Realm, mix with demon bloodlines (the origin of vampires) and leave. Ancestor demons cross into the Human World through Demon Gates and mix with humans. |
| Ancient period | Humans with very thin demon blood awaken supernatural abilities: the first **Taimanin clans**. A **pact of non-interference** is made between the two worlds; the clans hunt demons *or humans* who break it. Ancient gods are banished by a powerful witch and the Ancient Dragons. Demons become a secret in the human world. |
| c. 1180 `[SQ]`/Towako | End of the Heian period: the nine-tailed fox demon-god is sealed by Abe no Seimei's line (origin of the onmyoji plotline). |
| To the 2010s | Ordinary history, including both world wars; Japan ends up under stronger US influence. |
| Unknown | The **Fuuma clan** acts as the Taimanin clans' representative to Japan's government. |

## 2. Geopolitical backdrop

| Year | Confidence | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 2046 | approx. | **Taiwan Crisis** begins: China invades Taiwan. |
| 2047 | approx. | War spreads to Southeast Asia; the PLA lands on Okinawa. De facto China–US war. |
| 2048 | approx. | US forces bomb Beijing; a coup attempt in China is put down with Russian help. |
| 2049 | approx. | Ceasefire brokered by the EU. |
| 2050 | approx. | The USA reorganises as the **UFS** (United Federation of American-Pacific States); China as the **Chinese Union**. A cold war begins and both sides start mining demon-world technology for weapons. |
| 2052–2053 | order only | **Nomad** is founded in the UFS: publicly a multinational, secretly led by the vampire Edwin Black. |
| 2056 | approx. | **Tokyo Kingdom**, an artificial island in Tokyo Bay, is planned and built as a second city centre. |
| 2061–2062 | approx. | Tokyo Kingdom is abandoned half-built. The Chinese Union buys in through "Dragon Gate Capital", which becomes the **Dragon Gate** mafia. |
| 2062 `[VN]` / 2072 `[RPGX][AT][SQ]` | conflict | **Peninsula Conflict**: a UFS–Chinese Union proxy war destroys **Amidahara**, then Japan's third-largest city. Officially a nuclear strike; the lack of radiation suggests a failed magical experiment. Soon after, Demon Gates open across Japan and demons quietly settle the ruins. |
| 2064 | order only | **Section Three** (Public Safety Investigation Unit 3) is founded; the Taimanin begin to be organised under the government. |
| 2065 | `[ZERO]` solid; others inferred | **Gosha Village** is established and clans from across Japan migrate to it. |

## 3. The road to the first game

| Year | Confidence | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 2049 | approx. | **Asagi Igawa is born** (heir of the Igawa clan; father Shuzen Igawa, mother Kiku, grandmother the elder Seishu Hattori). The same year Kaede Shinganji is abducted by Edwin Black; her daughters **Kurenai** and **Felicia** result, but only Kurenai is recovered by her grandfather Gen-an. |
| 2053 | approx. | Murasaki Yatsu is born. |
| 2058 | approx. | **Sakura Igawa** is born; the sisters' mother dies shortly after. |
| 2064–2067 | order only | The Shinganji branch loses standing within the Fuuma clan (prologue of *Kurenai*). |
| 2067–2068 | order only | ***Taimanin Kurenai***: Kurenai goes rogue and Ayame becomes a vampire; the Nomad proxy "House of Greed" is destroyed. Fuuma corruption is exposed; Section Three's Nobushige Yamamoto meets the young Asagi. |
| 2068 | approx. | **Fuuma Revolt**: Danjo Fuuma, the first Taimanin captain, tries to seize Gosha when the government shifts its trust to the Igawa clan. Asagi (with Oboro Koukawa) defeats him. |
| 2068–2070 | conflict | **Shuzen Igawa** becomes the second captain and dies soon after. `[VN]`: dies "in the line of duty" amid clan infighting. `[RPGX][AT][SQ]`: murdered in the **Igawa Revolt** (2070) by his own mother Seishu Hattori and the conservative Igawa elders, who also try to kill Asagi; she wins with help from Shiranui Mizuki, Kuro Yatsu, the Koukawa clan and the surrendered Fuuma. |
| 2068 `[VN]` / 2073 `[RPGX][AT][SQ]` | conflict | **Koukawa Massacre**: the Koukawa clan is wiped out. Child survivor **Asuka** is taken in by Asagi. Clan heir **Oboro Koukawa** loses a duel and her body is taken; her soul escapes into another body (later the *Masked Taimanin*) while the corpse is remade as the villain **Oboro**. Culprit is Edwin Black in `[VN]`, the wraith lord Teutates disguised as Black in `[RPGX]`. |
| 2069 | approx. `[VN]` | **ONT Conflict**: Oboro and her Oboro Ninja Troops slaughter veteran Taimanin. Asagi, aged 20, kills her in Tokyo — then **retires** to live with her fiancé Kyousuke Sawaki and her sister. |
| 2070 | solid `[VN]` | **Gosha Academy** is completed to rebuild the ranks; Yamamoto serves as first headmaster. (`[RPGX][AT]` show a completed academy by 2068; `[ZERO]` says 2065.) |

## 4. Old World `[VN]` — the mainline decade

| Year | Entry | Events |
|---|---|---|
| **2072** (solid) | ***Taimanin Asagi*** + *Chaos Arena* | Oboro returns from the dead and runs the **Chaos Arena**, an illegal underground fighting ring owned by Nomad. Asagi (23) and Sakura are captured, altered by the demon-tech surgeon Sabato Kiryu, and forced to fight. Asagi kills Oboro — and with her Kyousuke, whose body Oboro had taken over. The corrupt Igawa elder who colluded with Nomad is dealt with. Asagi returns to duty as a squad captain and moves back to Gosha. |
| 2073 | — | **Shiranui Mizuki**, Asagi's senior and adviser, vanishes in Yomihara (captured by the incubus king Ryuuji Kuroi). In `[RPGX]` this happens in 2077 and the captor is the incubus king Kamadeva. |
| 2075 | ***Taimanin Murasaki*** | The Taimanin are formally folded into Section Three. Murasaki captures Kiryu, who defects and joins Gosha's science staff. |
| 2075 | *Summer Days* | A Dark Beast's cursed blood seals the arts of Asagi, Sakura and Murasaki for a while; Yamamoto orders a holiday; an opportunist demon tries his luck and dies. |
| **2076** (solid; retconned from 2073) | ***Taimanin Asagi 2*** | Missing-Taimanin investigation in **Tokyo Kingdom**. Dragon Gate has cloned Oboro and turned the captured Taimanin **Saya** into a bioweapon. Asagi first awakens her **inner demon** and kills Saya; the real Oboro (now a vampire) kills her own clone. Dragon Gate begins a slow decline; Nomad gains. |
| 2076 | *The Nightmare* | Asagi purges nearly all of the Asagi clones Dragon Gate had made. |
| 2077 | — | Asagi becomes **Taimanin Chief Commander** and **headmaster of Gosha Academy**. Sakura and Murasaki become teachers. Shiranui's husband dies searching for her, leaving their daughter Yukikaze an orphan. |
| 2077–2078 | *Mama wa Taimanin* | An incubus group targets retired Taimanin in the Kanto region. |
| **2078** | ***Taimanin Yukikaze*** | Yukikaze and Rinko Akiyama go undercover in the underground city **Yomihara** to find Shiranui. The mission is a trap laid by the politician Muneichi Yazaki; Asagi and Sakura extract them. |
| **2079** | ***Taimanin Yukikaze 2*** | Infiltration of **Seishu Academy**, a school for the elite that feeds the Kuroi faction. Yazaki is killed. Shiranui is found to have been turned. |
| 2080 | (flashback) | **Asuka's defection**: against orders she attacks Edwin Black alone, loses all four limbs, is saved by the Masked Taimanin and rebuilt with prosthetics by the UFS's DSO. Japan's ruling party collapses in scandal and terror; the outsider **Koujirou Asai** becomes prime minister. |
| 2081 | *Witch of Steel Annerose* | (order only, ±10 years) The Werewolf Incident in Amidahara. |
| **2082** (solid) | ***Taimanin Asagi 3*** | Prime Minister Asai frames the Taimanin for a political assassination, declares martial law and pushes union with the UFS. The **Kanda Brigade** occupies Gosha Village; about half the student body dies. Asai is revealed to be **Edwin Black**. Asagi (33) is captured and taken to Nomad's Palace of Darkness in Yomihara. **Two endings** — see document 01, section 3.2. |

## 5. New World `[ZERO]`

Dates from the official *Taimanin Saga* timeline.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2046–2053 | Same Taiwan Crisis / UFS / Chinese Union / Nomad backdrop. |
| 2064 | Section Three founded; Taimanin organised under the government. |
| 2065 | Gosha Village formed; clans migrate in. |
| 2067–2068 | Fuuma–Igawa power struggle; Danjo Fuuma killed. Nomad attacks a secret summit between Japan's government and Demon Realm lords, severing official human–demon contact. |
| 2069–2070 | Shuzen Igawa becomes chief, then dies on duty. **Yamamoto** takes over as acting commander — in this world *he* leads the Taimanin and the Igawa clan stays outside Gosha. |
| **2074** | ***Taimanin Asagi ZERO***: a younger, independent Asagi goes undercover in Gosha to find her captured sister; the villain is the naga chief **Kaliya**; Edwin Black / Vampire Asagi from the old world watch from behind the scenes, regarding this Asagi as a suitable vessel. |

## 6. Battle Arena `[TABA]`

Same as `[VN]` to 2082, then:

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2068 | Danjo Fuuma **survives** the revolt and flees to the UFS, joining Task Force G. |
| 2082 | Kotaro Fuuma (Danjo's son) graduates, leaves Gosha with his half-sister Tokiko and founds the **Fuuma Organization**, a rogue syndicate that captures and recruits Taimanin, UFS operatives and demons alike. *Asagi 3* is averted because Black turns his attention to Kotaro. |
| 2083–2085 | Episodic wars between the Fuuma Organization, Gosha, the DSO, Task Force G and Nomad. Kotaro kills his father and awakens an Evil Eye that can absorb other people and their powers. |
| 2086 | Joint assault on Nomad. Kotaro absorbs the dying Asagi to keep fighting Black; Black's reset collides with Kotaro's Evil Eye: the **TABA Reset**. |
| 2087 | Epilogue world: a short-lived Vampire Asagi offers Kotaro immortality; he refuses, and she reverts to human. |

## 7. RPG `[RPGX]`

Past differences from `[VN]`: Danjo **dies** in 2068 and the three-year-old Kotaro
surrenders the clan to Asagi, so the Fuuma live *inside* Gosha; the Igawa Revolt
happens (2070); Asagi never retires and never has the Kyousuke relationship; the
Koukawa Massacre is in 2073; Shiranui disappears in 2077; Nomad under Ingrid is a less
purely villainous force.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2082 | **Nisha Insurrection**: Gaiza Nisha, head of a Fuuma servant clan, rebels and tries to assassinate Asagi. Kotaro (a first-year who cannot use his clan's Evil Eye) helps stop it and is given command of the **Independent Commando Unit** (also "Taimanin Rangers"). |
| 2083 | Conflict with the **Internal Affairs Bureau**; the UFS cyborg "Library" defects to Gosha; a living weapon sent to assassinate Asagi is disarmed and sheltered. |
| 2084–2085 | Amidahara cases; a breakout from **Amidahara Prison** tied to a naga chief; Kotaro meets "Kuroto", the Edwin Black of the previous world. |
| 2086–2088 | Demon Realm politics (the Nine Nobles, the Wraith court of Teutates, the succubus succession), a second Igawa-elder attack on Gosha, an alliance with a Demon Realm viscounty. Students move up a grade each anniversary. |
| Side | **Dark Future** arcs: a timeline conquered by Brain Flayers sends refugees and adult versions of the cast back. |

## 8. Action `[AT]` and Squad `[SQ]`

- `[AT]`: Close to `[RPGX]` up to 2082, but the ONT Conflict and Chaos Arena *did*
  happen (so Asagi lost her fiancé), Sakura is still a student, and Shiranui was never
  captured and serves as deputy headmaster. In 2082 Yamamoto founds the **Taimanin
  Task Force** under Kotaro with Asagi, Sakura and Yukikaze as its core. Arcs: a
  stolen bioweapon and the zombie-style **CD Virus** outbreak on Kotrara Island;
  Asagi's former teacher **Toyo Momochi** as a rogue terrorist; a three-way contest
  for the Demon King's crown between Edwin Black, the inferno queen Astaroth and the
  Death Wraith King; a clone-framing plot run from inside the UFS.
- `[SQ]`: Shares the `[AT]` backstory. In 2082 demons in Tokyo Kingdom suddenly turn
  on the human population; Taimanin and UFS troops hold a last line at the Tokyo
  Kingdom Bridge. At one point the dormant Demon Gate under Gosha is briefly
  reactivated and mind-controlled demons swarm the village until the gate is destroyed.
- `[GOGO]`: After ten years of war Asagi brokers peace; Nomad dissolves; Edwin Black
  joins the academy staff; a **Labyrinth Dungeon** appears under Gosha and a misfit
  "Dungeon Squad" explores it.

## 9. Known retcons and contradictions

1. *Asagi 2* moved from 2073 to 2076; old dialogue in *Murasaki* ("the academy was
   founded four years ago") no longer fits and the wiki files it under an "abandoned
   timeline".
2. Peninsula Conflict: 2062 in `[VN]`, 2072 in `[RPGX]`/`[AT]`/`[SQ]`.
3. Koukawa Massacre: 2068 by Black `[VN]`; 2073 by Teutates posing as Black `[RPGX]`.
4. Danjo Fuuma: forced to flee or killed `[VN]`, survives `[TABA]`, dies `[RPGX][AT]`.
5. Shiranui: captured 2073 by Kuroi `[VN]`; 2077 by Kamadeva `[RPGX]`; never captured `[AT]`.
6. Gosha Academy founding: 2070 `[VN][TABA]`, by 2068 `[RPGX][AT]`, 2065 `[ZERO][SQ]`.
7. `[RPGX]` internal time is self-contradictory (the game says one or two years have
   passed while its events imply far more).
