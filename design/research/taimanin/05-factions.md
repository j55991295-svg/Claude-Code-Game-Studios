**The three-sided game.** *Battle Arena* formalised the setting as three playable
blocs — **Taimanin**, **UFS**, **Nomad/demons** — and that triangle is the best
single summary of the conflict. Each bloc is itself split.

## 2. The Taimanin side

### 2.1 Gosha Taimanin (対魔忍)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Nature | Japan's secret force of anti-demon ninja: clans united under the government |
| Size / base | About 10,000 people; Gosha Village; HQ at Gosha Academy |
| Reports to | **Section Three** (ministry of public safety), which funds, equips, researches targets and issues state missions |
| Work | Investigation, infiltration, espionage, hostage rescue, elimination of criminal demons and of humans abusing demon technology; upholding the old non-interference and secrecy rules |
| Method | Teams of two or three; offence-oriented (onmyoji are the defensive counterpart) |
| Secrecy | Existence is classified. Heavily fictionalised Taimanin media is deliberately released, so that real sightings are taken for a show |

**Policy and limits.** Pursue justice and protect the human world; solve inter-clan
disputes peacefully; cooperate with other agencies and allied nations. They will
assassinate without attempting capture if ordered. Their hands are tied against
"off-limits" targets — however corrupt or demonic — when the state benefits, which
is why detractors call them the government's dogs and why Nomad, a legal
corporation, cannot be attacked openly.

**Command structure**

| Rank | Role |
|---|---|
| Minister / director of Section Three | Approves large missions; can order new units formed |
| **Taimanin Chief Commander** | Top of the organisation; bridge between government and clans; also headmaster. Holders: Danjo Fuuma → Shuzen Igawa → (Yamamoto, acting) → **Asagi Igawa** |
| **Squad Captain** | Receives missions from the commander; leads or deploys a squad |
| Squad member | Full Taimanin; some squads require an awakened art |
| Academy student | Attached for support or training |
| Staff | Teachers, science and medical, equipment |

A clan seeing "its" member as Chief Commander tends to read it as *that clan ruling
the others*, which is the root of most internal conflict.

**Who counts as a Taimanin.** Anyone trained as a ninja who can use **Taima
particles** — an ability that appears when human and demon blood mixed somewhere in
the family line. "Taimanin" is older than the Gosha organisation; the army also
fields a **Taima Corps** of awakened ex-members with slight powers and ordinary
weapons.

**Allies (vary by continuity):** onmyoji, Exorcists, Magicians Association, DSO,
Vampire Hunters; in `[RPGX]` even the Beast Kingdom and a reformed Nomad.

### 2.2 Taimanin clans (家)

Clans are families with a long lineage and a hereditary pool of arts.

- **Governance:** a head (patriarch or matriarch) or small council; large clans add
  sub-clan heads, a **steward** (second-in-command who runs clan affairs, usually
  from a branch family) and **elders**, who can out-weigh the head.
- **Main and branch families:** the main line supplies the head, the branches the
  steward.
- **Bloodline arts:** each clan carries characteristic abilities, but an individual
  awakens **only one**. Marrying across clans widens the pool and dilutes the odds
  of the prized ability, which is why mixing was historically frowned upon.
- **Origins:** ancient Japan's wars with demons produced human–demon hybrids; as
  the blood thinned, new abilities appeared. Too weak to fight demons head-on, the
  early clans specialised in ambush — the origin of ninja technique. Some clans
  left Japan; some crossed into the Demon Realm and became the **Ryomanin**.

|---|---|---|---|

## 7. The Demon Realm

### 7.1 The Nine Nobles (魔界の9貴族)

An alliance of nine great **demon tribes**, each under a king or queen who holds a
"Lord" title, ruling the largest bloc of the realm on behalf of an **empty throne**.
The true Demon King is long dead with no heir. A composite monarchy that prefers
stability and non-interference with the human world, but is riddled with intrigue.
Official talks with human governments were wrecked by a Nomad-engineered attack.

| Title | Holder(s) | Tribe | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Demon King* | Vacant | — | Line extinct |
| **Chancellor** (宰相卿) | **Bismarck** | — | De facto ruler; commands the Hell Knights; opposes any lord who reaches for the crown |
| **Blood Lord** (紅血卿) | **Cara Cromwell** (her late father before her) | Vampires | Edwin Black's elder sister; capital Crimson City |
| **Corpse Lord** (死霊卿) | **Teutates** `[RPGX]`; an unnamed wraith king elsewhere | Wraiths | Ex-Hell Knight who killed the Flame Lord and took his lands; commands Revenant Knights; the great schemer of `[RPGX]` |
| **Phantom Lord** (幻夢卿) | Ereshkigal → Kamadeva † → **Shiranui Mizuki** `[RPGX]` | Incubi / succubi | Capital Flame End. A former Taimanin holding a noble seat is one of `[RPGX]`'s biggest twists |
| **Assassin Lord** (暗殺卿) | **Ceres** (true identity: the naga chief Vasuki) | — | No territory; operates in the capital; rivals the incubi over the brothel trade |
| **Wise Lord** (賢明卿) | **Marjaana** | Brain Flayer (not a demon) | Guards technology from both worlds; fortress Skyridge |
| **Flame Lord** (炎武卿) | Heliogabalus Gaap † | Inferno demons | Tried for the crown; killed; seat abolished |
| Remaining seats | Unknown | | Not yet named in any source |

**Hell Knights** (魔界騎士). Elite, human-like demon knights who enforce the
alliance. A knight's status **outranks nobility**: even a commoner who earns the
title may bring down a noble house. That makes knighthood the dream of anyone
wanting out of the realm's caste system. Entry is by apprenticeship to an existing
knight. They learn magic, weapon summoning and swordsmanship and keep a chivalric
code. Organised into **twelve Orders** ranked by power — Heavenly Sacred (Bismarck),
Dark Lord (Ceres), Black Dragon (formerly **Ingrid**), White Dragon (Carmella),
Gold Dragon (Marika Krishna), Silver Dragon (Belzebuth), and so on, apparently in
contrasting pairs.

**Other structures:** **demon tribes** (species-based communities with tribal
magic, which outsiders can join); the **Demon Realm Assassin Guild** ("demon
ninja"); **Revenant Knights** (the Corpse Lord's undead elite); independent kings
and queens outside the Nine.

## 9. Design notes — structures worth borrowing

1. **Every bloc has a hawk and a dove.** DSO vs Task Force G; Chief Commander vs
   elders; Chancellor vs ambitious lords; Black's Nomad vs Ingrid's Nomad. The
   player always has an ally *inside* an enemy faction and an enemy inside their own.
2. **Proxies everywhere.** Major powers rarely act directly; they act through
   gangs, shell companies and deniable units. That gives a natural enemy ladder:
   street gang → proxy → executive → patron.
3. **A legal enemy.** The main villain is a legitimate corporation the heroes are
   forbidden to attack. Constraint, not strength, drives the plot.
4. **A status that outranks birth** (Hell Knight) as a mobility path inside a
   caste society — a ready-made player or NPC career.
5. **Affiliation labels vs morality.** "Rogue" means "not ours", not "evil".
   Reputation should be tracked per faction, not on one good–evil axis.
6. **Clan inheritance rules** — one art per person from a hereditary pool; wider
   pool, lower odds — are a lineage/breeding system already written.
7. **Signature tech per bloc** (ninja arts / cyborgs / bioweapons / magic) gives
   each faction a distinct enemy roster and loot table.
