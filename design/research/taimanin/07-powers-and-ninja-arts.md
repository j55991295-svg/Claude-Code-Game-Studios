## 1. The three energies

Every supernatural act in the setting draws on one of three power sources. The
groups that use them each insist theirs is unrelated to the others; the setting's
own researchers suspect all three are the same thing seen through different
traditions.

| Energy | Japanese | Who uses it | What it does | Cost / risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Taima particles** ("anti-demon particles"; older name: *ki*) | 対魔粒子 | Taimanin; Western Exorcists (who call it a divine miracle); ninja beasts | Charges body, clothes and weapons. Basis of every ninja technique and Ninja Art. **Disrupts demon cells, magic and regeneration**, so a particle-charged hit does far more to a demon than the same hit from an ordinary weapon. | No corruption. Output can exceed the user's control (see Limiters). |
| **Spiritual energy** (*ki* / *qi*) | 気 | Onmyoji, vampire hunters, some Exorcists | Slight physical boost, sensing the flow of life, barriers, seals, projectiles, pacts with shikigami. Tied to spirits and gods. | Little health risk; needs a prepared **catalyst** (card, paper doll, ritual goods) — an onmyoji without one is helpless. |
| **Mana** ("magical power") | マナ / 魔力 | Demons, witches and magicians, demonologists, part-demon humans | Fuels all demon **magic** and even "biological" traits such as demon regeneration. Present in demons and thinly in the air; leaks into the human world through Demon Gates. | Finite — casters run dry. Humans cannot use it without demon blood or **demonization**. Some magic gives off **miasma**. |

Related facts:

- **Miasma** (瘴気) — a toxic vapour of the Demon Realm and a waste product of some
  magic. Thin miasma hangs over every gate city. It demonizes humans and animals
  (a stray cat becomes a tiger-sized beast), drives AI systems to ignore orders,
  and combined with radioactivity is lethal even to demons. Taimanin are immune.
- **Shared root.** Taimanin, onmyoji and exorcists all carry a thin strain of demon
  heritage; families thick with it produce the gifted. Taimanin specifically
  descend from human–demon unions in the deep past (see doc 06, *Aion*).
- **Theory.** Kenji Kotani's research `[VN]` treats Taima particles as "a catalyst
  that channels power from another dimension" — the Mother Sea. Demons see no real
  difference from their own magical power.
- **Detection.** The UFS builds particle detectors to spot Taimanin at range and
  researches artificial particles for weapons. Two-headed Black Dogs can smell them.

## 2. Ninja Arts (忍法, *ninpō*)

The signature power of the franchise: one innate, hereditary supernatural ability
per Taimanin.

### 2.1 Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **One per person** | Fixed by bloodline. A ninja can only awaken an art that has appeared somewhere in their ancestry. Two fire-art parents will never have a water-art child; a fire and a water parent may have either. |
| **Clans concentrate arts** | Generations of same-art marriages make that art likelier — hence clan signatures (Fuuma: Evil Eye; Igawa: Falcon and Shadow; Yatsu: Immortal Awakening; Mizuki: Water, rarely Lightning; Shinganji: Wind). |
| **Awakening is not guaranteed** | Nobody is born with an active art. Many Taimanin never awaken and fight on particle-boosted bodies and general techniques alone. Mental stress can trigger it; late bloomers awaken well into adulthood. |
| **An art is a family of techniques** | A fire-art user may have a fireball, a whip, a shield, healing flame or a last-ditch body-heat surge, and must discover and train each. Two users of one art can fight completely differently. |
| **Mastery branches** | Experts develop offshoots that even same-art users cannot copy — lightning used as a sensor, as a blade, as a decoy body. |
| **Weapon follows art** | After awakening, a ninja chooses a weapon that suits the art. Dangerously strong arts are issued a **Limiter**. |
| **Not magic** | Arts are powered by particles, and so cut through demon defences. Combination attacks can threaten even nobles who otherwise seem unkillable. |
| **Soul-bound** | Clones of Taimanin never awaken (the Chinese Union tried). A body occupied by a different soul uses the *soul's* art, not the body's. |

### 2.2 Getting a second art (exceptions)

Naturally impossible; every known case is surgery, ritual, theft or trickery.

| Route | Example |
|---|---|
| Transplant | Eyes carrying an Evil Eye grafted into a near-blind child; a demonologist secretly grafting a clan art into a boy who later awakens his own. Crude transplants pass on only fragments (for example immunity). |
| Ritual | The Shinganji clan's **God's Eye**: kill a bearer in a duel, bathe in the blood, and inherit the eye *and* the loser's art. Usable only in short, cursed transformations `[RPGX]`. |
| Theft | The Evil Eye *Demonic Gate* steals an art, stores it, and can run one stolen plus one stored art at once `[TABA]`. Edwin Black's energy drain takes abilities too. |
| Copy | Mirror Art copies one art at a time; Soul Art can briefly borrow the arts of people whose soul-fragments the user holds (up to five, weakly). |
| Carrier tricks | Storing a relative inside one's own body to use their art; scroll ammunition preloaded with other users' elemental arts. |
| Mixed blood | A half-demon usually learns the magic of the demon side first and may *also* awaken an unrelated art — people who assume the ice user has an ice art get surprised. |

### 2.3 Classification

The wiki's working taxonomy (fan-organised; the games do not print a master list):

- **Elemental** ("nature release") — external elemental attacks: fire, water, wind,
  earth, wood, metal, lightning, plus derived elements.
- **Exelemental** — everything else: body arts, mind arts, space-time, summoning.
- **Umbrella arts** — one name covering many unrelated abilities with a shared
  trigger (Evil Eye, Strength arts, Sensory arts).
- **Sub-arts** — weaker or narrower cousins (Elec < Lightning; Ignition < Fire).

Different arts often reach the same effect (five or six arts can make heat; water
and lightning can both make decoy bodies), so an enemy cannot infer the art from
one technique.

### 2.6 Limiters

A **Limiter** is a weapon-shaped regulator issued to ninja whose output outruns
their control. It is charged with the owner's particles and art (no ammunition),
is useless to anyone else, and trades peak power for aim and safety. Users can
deliberately overload one as a last resort, which destroys it. Yukikaze's twin
pistols are the best-known example; one pair was burnt out levelling part of an
airport. A user either grows out of the Limiter or — if output keeps climbing —
never does.

## 3. General ninja techniques (忍術, *ninjutsu*)

Learnable by any Taimanin. This is the baseline kit that an un-awakened ninja
relies on.

| Technique | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Particle control** | Core | Charge body or weapon. Prerequisite for everything else. |
| **Enhanced physique** | Passive | Top-athlete-or-better strength, reflexes, agility, endurance, pain tolerance. Not bullet-proof. |
| **Demonic detection** | Passive | A sixth sense for demonic presence in a building or room; cannot pinpoint; strong demons can mask themselves. Strengthened by the nine hand-signs (*kuji-kiri*), at the cost of leaving the user open. |
| **Demonic immunity** | Passive | Resistance to miasma, mana and demon cells. Fails when will or health gives out. |
| **Flash Step** (*shukuchi*) | Active | A burst of speed too fast to follow; may leave afterimages. |
| **Vibrational communication** | Utility | One-way directed speech only the target hears — replaces hackable radios. |
| **Clone techniques** | Active | Afterimages, body doubles or elemental decoys; only strong ninja, limited count. |
| **Razor Wind** | Active | A flying slash made by sheer speed, without a wind art. |
| **Inhuman Blood Art** (蠱血の術) | Advanced | Charging one's own blood with life force so it poisons vampires. |
| **Bedroom arts** | Advanced | Learnable seduction and resistance techniques, used by infiltrators both to control a target and to withstand capture. |
| **Hand-signs** | Modifier | Optional boost to other techniques. |

**Taught fundamentals** at Gosha Academy: unarmed combat (*taijutsu*), thrown
weapons (*shurikenjutsu*), sword (*kenjutsu*) to a passable standard, then one
chosen weapon. A few go on to "anti-demon killing techniques" at peak human level —
kick-based **Blade Legs** and rock-breaking fists — usually ninja whose art is not
itself a weapon.

**Weapon schools** (*ryū*): Omine (the common style), Ittō (single sword; the
Akiyama dojo), Nen (thrusting), Shinken, Nitō (two swords), Iaijutsu (draw cuts),
Sōjutsu (spear), Naginatajutsu, Jōjutsu (staff, non-lethal). **Clan styles** (Fuuma
arts, Igawa arts) were secret before unification.

**Field disciplines:** disguise, silent entry, water work, espionage and
code-breaking, escape and lock work, tactics and political manipulation.

**Everyday kit:** communicator, Taimanin suit, antidote kit, kunai and shuriken,
wingsuit, face mask.

## 4. Awakening — the Inner Demon (覚醒, *kakusei*)

Separate from awakening an art. A very few Taimanin can touch "something" that is
both inside them and outside the world, and transform.

- **Effect.** Large gains in speed, strength, damage resistance and art power —
  enough to trouble a demon noble. Hair, eyes and skin change; claws, horns or
  pointed ears may appear. The form is individual.
- **Cause.** The mix of human and demon blood, plus a very specific mental or
  physical trigger. All Taimanin have the potential; almost none reach it.
- **The entity.** It speaks to its host in dreams or awake, knows things the host
  does not (the Mother Sea, the Aion, gods), usually despises humans, and pushes
  the host to use it.
- **Risk.** Personality shifts toward the demon while transformed. Stay too long
  and the host's mind is erased permanently.
- **Secrecy.** Outsiders mistake it for demonization, so those who have it hide it.

Asagi's "Kage" is the reference case ([09](09-asagi-igawa.md)).

## 5. Demon magic (魔法, *mahō*)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Source | Mana, plus stamina. Casters run out. |
| Who | All demons have some potential; some species share one innate magic (inferno demons cast fire, wraiths raise the dead). Witches and magicians learn several types. |
| Humans | Cannot cast without recent demon ancestry or demonization. Chasing magic is the usual road to self-demonization, madness and narcissism. |
| Learned and inherited | Children of witches are gifted; others apprentice to a master. |
| Counter | Particle-charged attacks interrupt spells and outweigh magical defences. |
| Cost | Strong magic needs sacrifices (souls) or carries personal risk. Powerful witches are slow casters. |
| Hell technology | The fusion of studied magic with human engineering — the root of most advanced weapons and medicine in the setting. |

**Magic types** (wiki umbrella terms, grouped):

| Group | Types |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | Elemental (fire, ice, lightning…), Energy, Darkness, Light, Blood (vampires), Poison (naga), Void (see below) |
| **Space and force** | Gravity (Edwin Black; shields, vortexes and ultimately the world Reset), Transfer (teleport and summon), Levitation, the labyrinth spell *Crossroad of Confusion* |
| **Mind and soul** | Mind, Illusion, Dream (incubi and succubi), Control (command lesser creatures), Soul Binding, Divination, Demon Eye (demon counterpart of the Evil Eye) |
| **Life and death** | Healing (weak on oneself), Drain (life, mind, even abilities), Necromancy (wraiths, liches), Curse (reflects harm back), Spirit possession |
| **Making and changing** | Alchemy, Transmutation (petrify, transform), Barrier (walls and zones that buff or debuff), Spirit magic (borrow a lesser spirit's power through a medium), Tracking |
| **Oddities** | Maid magic (housework spells with sinister uses), Dance magic (give or take vitality), Chaos magic (recombine matter), Demonic Embroidery (glowing tattoos that grant strength) |

**Void power** (虚無の力) is the top-tier exception: obtained only by consuming a
*Residue of Darkness*; it nullifies abilities, regeneration and barriers. Its
bearers call themselves vessels of the void.

**Magical contracts** deserve separate mention because they are how the Demon
Realm keeps order among beings who trust only strength: a contract binds both
parties to stated terms on pain of injury or death, can hold even nobles and
gods, may carry a time limit, and can be released early only by the holder. In
the human underworld the same magic appears as slave contracts and branded
"crests" that make escape impossible and may also work on the mind.

## 6. Onmyoji and Exorcist techniques

**Onmyōdō** — the onmyoji toolkit, powered by spiritual energy and built on the
five-phase theory (*gogyō*):

| School | Does |
|---|---|
| Wood | Protection from demons; physical strengthening |
| Fire | Purifying attacks |
| Earth | Summoning — demons and shikigami |
| Metal | Alchemical formulas that change the nature of things and work on the soul |
| Water | Weakening demons; healing |

Sub-techniques include elemental spells, **barriers** (weaken or bar entrants, seal
objects, disguise a site), **seals** and **ofuda** talismans. Everything needs a
catalyst card, so onmyoji are slower to start and considered the *defensive* arm
to the Taimanin's offensive one.

**Shikigami** are the onmyoji answer to a Ninja Art: once in a lifetime an onmyoji
contracts a spirit or demon, summoned through a paper doll bearing their mark.
The two then share pain, damage and life — if one dies, so does the other. Some
abilities depend on the caster's karma. Trading one's soul for more power is
forbidden.

**Exorcists** (EU) fight much as Taimanin do, with art-like gifts and ritual
weapons, under heavy religious framing; some families share blood with Taimanin
clans. Angels are spoken of but never confirmed.

## 7. Corruption mechanics

How characters are changed against their will is a core part of the franchise's
plots, and it is systematic enough to model.

| Mechanic | What it does | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|
| **Demonization** (魔化) | Turns a human or animal into a demon, altering mind as well as body. Triggers: miasma, drugs, implanted demon cells, or being turned by a vampire, wraith or incubus. Partial cases gain regeneration, extra limbs or magic. | Only at an early stage, by a demonologist replacing affected cells. |
| …on Taimanin | Particles resist it. A weakened or dead Taimanin can still be turned, usually becoming *weaker* overall — losing particle strength while gaining mana affinity and new features. | — |
| **Body modification** (改造) | Demonologist surgery with hell-tech and drugs: added strength, altered or added body parts, and forced heightening of physical sensitivity used to break captives. Takes hours to days; crude work can cost sanity or life. | Generally permanent; managed with willpower or medication. |
| **Spirit possession** | Implanting a lesser demon or spirit to boost or revive a body, at the cost of the original ego. | Rarely. |
| **Brainwashing and mind control** | By art, magic, drug, machine or implanted chip. | Varies. |
| **Contracts and crests** | See §5. | By the holder, or on expiry. |
| **Limb replacement with demon cells** | For a Taimanin, either demonization starts or their own particles attack the graft. | — |

## 9. Cosmic-scale powers

- **Gravity and the Reset.** Edwin Black's gravity magic can collapse the human and
  demon worlds into each other; the fused mass falls into the Mother Sea and
  "splashes" into new worlds. He keeps his memories across the Reset and can carry
  others or their powers with him, but cannot choose what the new worlds are.
- **Singularity.** A timeline split that creates a world where a fated event did
  not happen. It can save a doomed world or do the opposite; results are
  unpredictable.
- **Dimensional travel**, **Otherworld Gates** and their costs:
  see [03 — World and Locations](03-world-and-locations.md).

## 10. Conflicts and uncertainty

- The art taxonomy (elemental / exelemental, umbrella arts, "derived" links) is
  largely the wiki's inference; question marks in its own tables are kept in spirit
  here by wording such as "possibly".
- Whether Falcon Art is speed or time manipulation is stated both ways.
- Whether Taima particles, spiritual energy and mana are one thing is an open
  in-universe question, deliberately.
- Kotaro Fuuma's art differs by continuity: an ability-stealing Evil Eye `[TABA]`;
  an eye that will not open `[RPGX]` `[AT]`.
- Oboro's art: Cicada Shell belongs to the original soul; the revived body was
  *given* a hypnotic seal.

## 11. Design notes — structures worth borrowing

1. **One innate gift + a shared learnable kit.** Clean split between a character's
   unique "class skill tree" (the art) and universal skills anyone can buy.
2. **Awakening as a progression gate**, with real odds of never passing it and a
   respectable path for those who do not (weapon mastery, onmyoji retraining).
3. **Three resource pools with different economics**: an internal, safe one
   (particles); a prepared-consumable one (catalysts); a powerful, corrupting one
   (mana).
4. **Hard counter triangle already implied by lore**: particles beat magic and
   regeneration; technology detects and jams particle users; magic and corruption
   beat unprotected humans and machines (miasma scrambles AI).
5. **Limiters** — equipment that *lowers* stats in exchange for control, with an
   overload button that destroys the item.
6. **A transformation with a sanity clock** (the Inner Demon).
7. **Heredity as content**: bloodlines, clan arts and marriage as a breeding or
   legacy system; art theft and transplant as rare, costly exceptions.
8. **Corruption as a second health bar** with staged, mostly irreversible states.
9. **Lateral growth**: tiers are wide, and advancement is mostly new applications
   of one power rather than bigger numbers.
