# 06 — Races and Species

> Part of the Taimanin research reference. See [README](README.md) for sources and
> continuity tags. Powers are explained in
> [07 — Powers, Ninja Arts and Magic](07-powers-and-ninja-arts.md); how these beings
> appear as opponents is in [10 — Enemies and Bestiary](10-enemies-bestiary.md).

## 1. How the setting sorts living things

"Demon" (悪魔; also *yōkai*, *mazoku*) is not a species. It means *anything native to
the Demon Realm* — people, animals, plants. The fan wiki catalogues about 200
kinds; the useful cut is by **origin**:

| Origin | What belongs here | Shared traits |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Realm** | Humans; the human bloodlines with powers (Taimanin, onmyoji, exorcists); ninja beasts; a few native spirits and ghosts | No magic without demon blood; short lives; technology |
| **Demon Realm** | Every "demon": humanoids, demi-humans, beasts, undead, plants | Mana, regeneration, long life, strength-first culture; **all are weak to Taima particles** |
| **Otherworld** | Spirits, gods and devils, ancient dragons, fairies (possibly), shinigami | Cross realms without a gate; cannot be truly killed, only driven home; weakened outside their realm |
| **Outside** | Aion (the Mother Sea); Brain Flayers and their creatures | Dimensional travel; gravity and portal mastery |
| **Made** | Cyborgs, clones, synthetics, homunculi, chimeras, golems, ghouls | Built by humans, demons or Brain Flayers — see §6 |

Two rules cut across all of them:

- **Everything interbreeds.** Humans, demons and Otherworld beings produce fertile
  offspring. Several whole demon species are said to descend from an Otherworld
  ancestor (vampires from the Aion, naga from a snake god, dragonfolk from
  dragons), and the Taimanin exist because of it.
- **Species can be changed.** Miasma or implanted cells demonize humans; a vampire
  or wraith can *turn* another demon, producing mixed beings such as a vampire-oni.

Demon society ranks by strength. The wiki's rough ladder: **low** (orcs, goblins,
imps, ogres, doppelgangers, most beastmen) → **middle** (oni proper, naga,
arachne, dark elves, inferno demons) → **high / ruler class** (vampires, wraiths,
incubi and succubi, rakshasa, valkyries, liches) → **noble** individuals and
tribal **Kings / Queens** → the Nine Nobles ([05 — Factions](05-factions.md)).

## 2. Human-realm peoples

| People | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Humans** (人間) | Physically weaker and shorter-lived than demons but numerous, organised and inventive. Slowly adapting to low mana since the gates opened. Can become cyborgs, bioweapons or demons. |
| **Taimanin** | Humans with ancient demon (Aion-line) ancestry, able to generate Taima particles. Not a separate species — a set of bloodlines organised as ninja clans. See docs 05 and 07. |
| **Ryoumanin** | A branch of the same stock that migrated to the Demon Realm long ago. Thinly documented. |
| **Onmyoji / Exorcists** | Human bloodlines with the same faint demon heritage, trained in different traditions (spiritual energy; faith). |
| **Hybrids** (雑種; "half-demons") | Children of a human and a demon. Usually inherit the demon side's innate magic first; if the human parent was a Taimanin they may also awaken a Ninja Art. Half-vampires are **dhampirs**. Often distrusted by both sides. |
| **Ninja beasts** (忍獣) | Animals bred and raised by Taimanin that carry particles and near-human intelligence — dogs, cats, birds and others that fight alongside their handlers. Some elite ones are said to have arts of their own `[RPGX]`. |
| **Tsukumogami** | Long-used objects that became people resembling their owners. |
| **Possessor spirits** | A harmless tribe of bodiless ghosts living in the human world; nameless and genderless until adulthood. |

## 3. Demon-realm humanoids

### 3.1 Ruler-class species

| Species | Core traits | Strengths | Weaknesses and limits | Society |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vampire** (吸血鬼) | Undead-like, cold-skinned, human in appearance; descended from the Aion. | Near-immortal regeneration; blood, illusion and transformation magic; their blood heals and turns. Draining a victim fully leaves a **ghoul** servant. | Sunlight halves the strength of all but the pure-blooded; killable only with particles or special tools. Garlic irritates; silver and crosses are nearly useless against anything of rank. | Grades: turned vampires → dhampirs → pure-blood / ancestor vampires. Hereditary enemies of wraiths. Blood Lord Cara Cromwell's hidden Vlad Kingdom; Edwin Black is the best-known individual. |
| **Wraith** (レイス) | Ageless undead-type demons, grey-skinned, pointed ears. | Raise corpses as zombies, ghosts and skeletons and steer them with a special miasma; can turn strong beings into wraiths. | Bound to their lord at the cellular level — obedience is pleasurable and instinctive, so the tribe behaves like a hive. | Ruled by the **Corpse Lord** (Teutates); officers are Revenant Knights. |
| **Death Wraith** (屍鬼族) | A rarer cousin: bodiless immortals that wear corpses "like hermit crabs" and can fuse several into a soul-eating mass. | Possession; effectively unkillable without soul-level attacks. | Seldom aggressive unless challenged. | Own king. So rare that veterans have never fought one. |
| **Incubus / Succubus** (淫魔) and **dream demons** (夢魔) | Beautiful, intelligent, strongly magical. | Enter and shape dreams; drain life force; subliminal influence, disguise and infiltration; some can morph their bodies. | Avoid direct combat. | Once among the Demon Realm's great races; an incubus-king faction works through Nomad and human corporations. Individuals range from predatory to kindly; some of the young reject the label altogether. |
| **Lich** | Undead sorcerer. | High magic, near-immortality, raises the dead. | Slow recovery. | Solitary. |

### 3.2 Oni (鬼) — a genus, not a species

Horned, muscular, human-like; sell their services as mercenaries and guards;
favour traditional weapons; fond of drink and fighting but often reasonable.
Middle and high oni wear **noh masks** that regulate their violent emotions, and
the strongest can enter a **berserk** state.

| Rank | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| High | **Rakshasa** (羅刹天) | Honourable battle-seekers of extreme speed; rival the strongest Taimanin. |
| High | **Valkyrie** | All-female "warrior maidens" of the icy far lands; armour-like natural scales. |
| Middle | **Oni proper / Masurao** | The mercenary backbone — for example the Tokyo Kingdom band Kibushu. |
| Middle | **Frost Phantom** (霜の鬼神) | Strongest ice magic; drain body heat on touch; need cold to fight at full strength. |
| Low | **Ogre** | Very strong, simple, aggressive; as strong as a skilled Taimanin but below the elite. Often enslaved as arena fighters and guards. |
| Low | **Goblin** | Smallest oni; shunned for aggression; win by numbers. |

### 3.3 Beastmen (獣人) — also a genus

Look human with small animal features, and shift to a half-beast or full-beast form
for combat (much weaker untransformed). Mammals and a few birds only. Low status,
but they can pass as human without a disguise spell, so they work freely in the
human world. Notably strong healing; keen senses.

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Werewolf / dog demons** | Powerful, nearly extinct; from the Land of Ice and Snow. |
| **Nekomata** | Cat demons, playful and usually friendly; can gather in small armies. (**Cait Sith** look similar but are fairies.) |
| **Kamaitachi** | Weasel-eared wind users with scythes; now rare. |
| **Tigers, bears, monkeys, snow and silver variants** | Regional tribes; the Beast Kingdom family is a Tokyo Kingdom power. |
| **Divine beasts** | Rare, far stronger variants. |

### 3.4 Other humanoids

| Species | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Common humanoid demon** | Unnamed majority people of the Demon Realm; the most human in looks and outlook. Ingrid is one. |
| **Orc** (オーク) | The setting's stock low-rank demon: human-level intelligence, superhuman strength, servile toward the strong. All male; reproduce with other species; bodily fluids are aphrodisiac, which is why the underworld employs them as "trainers". Individuals vary widely — shopkeepers, information brokers, electronics experts, pacifists. **High Orcs** lead about a hundred each under a war chief; **Flame Orcs** cast fire through sealing stones. Orcs seen in the human world are the realm's weaklings. |
| **Troll** | Giant, primitive, fast-healing; prison guards and muscle. |
| **Elf / Dark Elf** | Elves are near-human magic users; dark elves are the Demon Realm branch, drawing on the sealed World Tree forest. |

| **Arachne** | Spider people of a warm region; webs drain life force. |
| **Dragonfolk** (竜人) | Descendants of dragon bloodlines; reclusive and coveted for their power. |
| **Inferno demon** (獄炎魔族) | Fire-immune people of the Burning Plains; the strong cast fire, the rest use technology. |
| **Yuki-onna** | All-female ice users; most want to be left alone. |
| **Doppelganger** | All-female, low-rank; copy a person's appearance *and memories*. |
| **Harpy**, **Merfolk**, **Minotaur**, **Centaur** | Regional demi-humans; merfolk lure and eat humans. |
| **Dryad** | Plant people living off the land's mana; isolated and dwindling. |

| **Ryomen Sukuna** | Ancient paired demons that fuse into one two-headed, four-armed fighter; almost extinct. |

## 4. Demon-realm beasts and monsters

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Lust beasts** (淫獣) | Umbrella term for tentacled and similar creatures that subdue prey with neurotoxins; partly descended from a transformed incubus tribe. Kept by criminals as tools for breaking captives. |
| **Black / Blood / Night Dogs** | Bull-sized two-headed hounds; orcs hunt with them; they **track Taima particles**. |
| **Dragon** | Bestial, winged, devastating; feared even at home. |
| **Hydra** | Many-headed water serpent; nearly unkillable beside water, weaker on land. |
| **Behemoth** | Gigantic, fast, winged; raised by lords to raze enemy land. |
| **Demon insects** (魔蟲) | From fly-sized swarms to humanoid types; bred for war. |
| **Gargoyle** | Stone-disguised guards, said to be an ancient magician's creation. |
| **Mimic** | Chest-shaped ambush predator. |
| **Cave Monster** | A single organism filling a cave system, with a hypnotic gas and worker drones `[RPGX]`. |
| **Void Slime** | Fungus-like parasite born from a dead lord's remains; spreads by spores `[RPGX]`. |

**Undead** (a type, not a lineage): **ghosts** (souls that did not move on —
poltergeists, will-o'-wisps, scarecrows), **shadows / shades** (souls corrupted by
miasma), **necroids** (skeletons, from mindless up to spell-casting *deadminds*),
**death knights** (skeletons that kept their swordsmanship), **zombies** (raised by
wraiths), **ghouls** (drained by vampires).

## 5. Otherworld and outside beings

| Being | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Spirits** (精霊) | Nature spirits and embodied concepts — *not* the dead. From lesser spirits that live in stones (used in spirit magic) up to gods. Travel between realms without gates. An onmyoji's **shikigami** is a role a spirit or demon takes, not a species. |
| **Gods / Devils** (神) | The highest spirits; once ruled the Demon Realm before being driven out, and still feared by demons. Above any noble. Staying in the lower worlds costs vast energy and cuts their strength; if emptied they are pulled home rather than killed. |
| **Changeling** | A fallen god reduced to its one function: replacing people with something else. |
| **Ancient Dragon** | "God-killers", intelligent, shape-adaptive, possibly Otherworld-born; ancestors of dragons and dragonfolk. |
| **Shinigami** | Invisible, benevolent guides of the dead — for humans and demons alike. |
| **Fairy** | Mostly harmless, sometimes mischievous; reborn from a magical source; bond souls with humans and grow strong through the bond. |
| **Angel** | Believed in by exorcists; never confirmed. |
| **Aion** (アイオーン) | "Inhabitants of the realm that envelops the world": masters of gravity who came from the Mother Sea in antiquity, fathered the first vampires in the Demon Realm and the Taimanin lines among humans, and vanished. Unclear whether a people or a single being. Linked to Edwin Black and to the Inner Demons. |
| **Brain Flayers** | Squid-like dimensional raiders, thousands of years old, heavily self-modified. They eat brains, leaving a zombie servant and taking its knowledge. Their civilisation is far ahead of humanity but stagnant: they loot technology and resources world by world. Main threat of `[TABA]` and the Dark Future of `[RPGX]`. Use **tentacle worms** and "brain dogs" as beasts. |

## 7. Conflicts and uncertainty

- The wiki's "genus" groupings (oni, beastmen) and its rank ladder are editorial
  syntheses; the games name species case by case.
- Elves' canon status rests on a separate Lilith title treated as canon by `[TABA]`.
- Whether the Aion are one being or many is deliberately unresolved.
- Angels are unconfirmed in-universe.
- Several species appear in only one event or card set `[RPGX]` `[TABA]`.

## 8. Design notes — structures worth borrowing

1. **Sort by origin, not by "race".** Origin decides the rules a being follows
   (what kills it, how it travels, what it is weak to), which makes a clean
   top-level tag for a bestiary and for damage types.
2. **Genus → species → rank.** "Oni" from goblin to rakshasa gives one visual and
   cultural family that scales from trash mob to boss.
3. **A universal weakness plus species-specific ones.** Everything demonic is weak
   to the heroes' own energy; vampires add sunlight, frost phantoms need cold,
   hydras need water, beastmen are weak untransformed.
4. **"Cannot be killed, only banished"** for the top tier — a built-in reason for
   recurring bosses.
5. **Low-rank species with individuals who break type** (the orc shopkeeper, the
   friendly slime) keep a hostile-by-default bestiary from being flat, and supply
   merchants, informants and recruits.
6. **Three manufacturing philosophies** — machine, biological, magical — give three
   distinct enemy families from one "artificial" category, each with its own flaw.
7. **Conversion between categories** (turning, demonization, cloning, possession)
   doubles as a status-effect system and a source of tragic enemies.
