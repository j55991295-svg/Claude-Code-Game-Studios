## 1. Cosmology at a glance

```text
Mother Sea (higher-dimensional void; every universe is born from it)
 ├─ Taimanin universe (one of very many near-copies)
 │    ├─ Human Realm  ── Demon Gates ──  Demon Realm ("Dark World", Makai)
 │    ├─ Dream World   (mindscape reachable by incubi/succubi and some tech)
 │    └─ Cyberspace    (networked virtual space; can be entered directly)
 ├─ Otherworld(s)      (gap-realms of gods, true devils and spirits; also used
 │                      loosely for "any other universe")
 └─ Other Lilith universes (crossover games, bad-ending worlds, future worlds)
```

| Layer | Nature | Travel |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Realm** (人間界) | Alternate-history Earth, c. 2072–2090. Big cities are low-key cyberpunk; small towns just look modern. | — |
| **Demon Realm** (魔界, *Makai*) | Birthplace of all demons. Not a fiery hell: it has continents, climates, day and night. Tech level is late-medieval kingdoms plus powerful magic; some stolen human tech. High ambient **mana**, traces of toxic **miasma**. | **Demon Gates** — permanent, always-open anchors. Natural or artificial. Cannot currently be closed. |
| **Otherworld** (異界) | Home of gods, true devils and spirits. | **Otherworld Gate** — needs a Philosopher's Stone or a being from that realm, plus a large supply of human souls to hold it open. A stable one could end the world. |
| **Mother Sea** | Fifth-dimensional medium outside the universes. | Reached by manipulating gravity; doing so destroys or resets a universe. |
| Other universes | Parallel worlds. | Brain Flayer portals; resets; rare artefacts. An ungated contact creates a local pocket of warped gravity and space. |

**The pact.** In antiquity humans and demons agreed not to interfere with each
other's world. The clans that became the Taimanin enforced it on both sides. The
pact is now dead — broken, by the setting's own account, mainly from the *human*
side, through greed for demon technology.

**What gates do to a place.** Wherever an active gate is found, demons and outlaws
from both sides settle around it for the trade. Governments write the city off.
These are the setting's **"Hell Cities" / "Dark Cities"**. Mana leaking through the
gates has also slowly adapted the whole human species to low levels of magic.

## 3. The Demon Realm

- At least one great landmass. **Centre**: the capital **King's Rock** (a city of about
  a million; seat of the empty Demon King's throne). **North**: the domains of the
  Blood Lord (capital **Crimson City**, on the coast of the Winter Sea), the former
  Flame Lord (the Gaap Kingdom) and, north-east, the Phantom Lord (capital **Flame
  End**). **West / "Midwest"**: small forest states in constant skirmish. **South**:
  barbarian lands. **Far edge**: the **Land of Ice and Snow** (frost phantoms,
  dwarves, werewolves, valkyries).
- Most settlements are villages or medieval-style kingdoms with little reach beyond
  their walls. Wilderness is roamed by beasts and bandit tribes. Not knowing local
  custom usually ends in enslavement.
- Cities grow up around gates: **Gate City**, a rulerless border trade hub on the far
  side of Yomihara's gate; the **Ruined Castle Gate** opposite Amidahara's.
- The **Wise Lord Domain** has no real capital — a few towns and the fortress
  **Skyridge**, stone outside and highly technological within, ruled by the same
  non-demon queen for 10,000 years.
- **Vlad Kingdom** — hidden vampire kingdom of Cara Cromwell (location differs by
  continuity).

Rulership and politics are in [05 — Factions](05-factions.md).

## 5. Design notes — structures worth borrowing

1. **Paired worlds joined by fixed gates.** Each gate has a human-side city and a
   demon-side city that grew rich on the same trade. A gate is an *economy*, not
   just a door.
2. **Three hub cities, three governance models**: corporate rule by one syndicate
   (Yomihara), a cartel balance of five gangs (Tokyo Kingdom), and a neutral
   guild-enforced order (Amidahara). Each implies a different reputation system.
3. **A safe home base that is hard to reach** (Gosha) contrasted with open,
   dangerous cities.
4. **Gates destabilise their surroundings** — a ready in-fiction reason for
   dungeons, anomalies and crossovers near any gate.
5. **Dormant gates** under ordinary places (Gosha's own) as late-game escalation.
6. **Layered travel difficulty**: gate (routine) → otherworld gate (needs rare
   materials and a terrible cost) → Mother Sea (world-ending). A natural tiering
   for a multiverse game's "Gates".
