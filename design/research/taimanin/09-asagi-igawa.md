## 2. Personality

**Core.** Mature, professional, responsible and self-accountable. Calm veteran with a
dry sense of humour. Demands discipline on duty and insists on rank and honorifics —
even from her sister — but is relaxed and playful at home.

**Defining trait: willpower.** Sources repeatedly describe her resolve as bordering on
superhuman. Captured, she taunts her captors rather than pleading. In the visual novels
she only breaks in bad-ending branches, never on the true route.

**As a fighter.** Enjoys the fight and taunts enemies, but stays disciplined. Kills
without hesitation when required; spares opponents who surrender honestly. To demons
she is "elegant yet terrifying" — her name alone is a threat.

**As a leader.** Believes people come before rules. Has pushed reforms that turned
feuding clans into one force. Increasingly strategic and long-term in outlook; holds
back in the field so the next generation gets experience. Privately worries that age
will dull her edge, which is why she pushes juniors hard.

**As family.** The responsible older sister: does the housework and the finances
because Sakura is chaotic. Motherly to friends, students and her adopted children — to
a fault: she sees Asuka and Kosuke as her children first and as Taimanin second, which
is the root of her conflict with Asuka.

**Private side.** Frustrated by desk work and budgets; unwinds with a drink at home in
the evening and can overdo it when out (a habit picked up from her relative Hakuou
Igawa). More reserved about romance than her sister. After Kyousuke's death she turns
down every proposal, saying she is too busy; the real reason is guilt and a fear that
anyone close to her will be made a target. Clan elders press her to produce an heir.
When she does love someone she is devoted and affectionate.

**In youth.** Arrogant, abrasive and rebellious; distrusted the clans' unification and
went through an independent, near-rogue phase. Refused to draw her father's sword and
fought with kicks alone until she could no longer treat fights lightly.

**When broken (bad-ending branches).** Becomes needy and submissive — the deliberate
inverse of her normal self. Her demon and vampire forms are colder, superior and
contemptuous of human ties.

**Comedy version `[GOGO]`.** Irresponsible, tongue-tied and overconfident; deliberately
lets demons in so that everyone trains by brawling daily.

## 4. Biography

### 4.1 Youth (2049–2069)

Trained in the sword by her father; awakened her art early; recognised young as the
likely strongest Taimanin of the modern era. Friends among her seniors included
Shiranui and Kuro. Introduced around 2067 to Yamamoto of Section Three as the Igawa
clan's showpiece. In the **Fuuma Revolt** (2068) she defeated the Fuuma leader Danjo —
then stopped her own clan from taking revenge on the Fuuma as a whole. Her father
became Taimanin captain and died soon after (see timeline for the two versions); she
took up his sword. After the **Koukawa Massacre** she took in the child Asuka, placing
her with the Yatsu clan to shield her from village suspicion.

In the **ONT Conflict** (2069) the returned "Oboro" and her troops ambushed and killed
many veterans; only Asagi could beat her, and did. When the government still took no
action against Nomad, Asagi refused both the clan headship and the captaincy and
**retired** to Tokyo with Kyousuke and Sakura.

### 4.2 *Taimanin Asagi* (2072)

An Igawa elder informs her that Oboro is alive and killing Taimanin again. Asagi and
Sakura trace her to the **Chaos Arena**. Returning home, Asagi finds that Oboro has
been regrown from demonic cells implanted in Kyousuke and has absorbed him. Oboro's new
art lets her puppeteer an opponent's body; Asagi is captured.

In the arena physical torture fails, so her captors switch to sexual humiliation as
the means of breaking her will. A Nomad surgeon, **Sabato Kiryu**, applies demon-world
**body modification** to both sisters that multiplies physical sensitivity (the
franchise's notorious "3,000 times" figure). The adult content of the game is this
captivity; fights are rigged with Oboro's control art and losses are punished.
(Summarised here, not detailed.)

The finale is a staged duel in which the sisters are meant to kill each other. They
fake it; Sakura uses her hidden **Shadow Art** to break Oboro's concentration and Asagi
cuts Oboro down — knowingly killing what is left of Kyousuke. Edwin Black, watching,
lets them leave as a "reward". The elder is exposed as Nomad's collaborator and dealt
with. Recordings of the arena circulate on the dark net, where they are taken for
fakes; among demons they cement her reputation. Asagi abandons civilian life, moves to
the Igawa Estate in Gosha, and forms a squad with Kuro and Sakura.

### 4.3 *Taimanin Asagi 2* (2076)

Undercover in **Tokyo Kingdom**, investigating missing Taimanin, the sisters are
defeated by **Saya** — a captured Taimanin whom Dragon Gate drove to suicide to awaken
her inner demon, producing a childlike, monstrous bioweapon — and held by a **clone of
Oboro** who intends the same for them. In captivity Asagi begins hearing her own
**inner demon** ("Kage", the Shadow). Believing Sakura dead, she partly gives in to it,
kills Saya and reverts. She recognises Saya as a victim. Afterwards she destroys almost
all of the Asagi clones Dragon Gate made, fearing each could awaken the same power.

### 4.4 Commander and headmaster (2077–2081)

Yamamoto names her **Chief Commander** and **headmaster of Gosha Academy**. Her field
work becomes mentoring: she takes Asuka, Sakura and Murasaki on missions. She approves
Yukikaze and Rinko's Yomihara mission, has misgivings, investigates, and personally
extracts them when it goes wrong; later covers up the collateral damage of their
follow-up operation. Asuka's unauthorised attack on Edwin Black (2080) leaves Asagi
believing she has lost a daughter.

### 4.5 *Taimanin Asagi 3* (2082)

- Reunites with Asuka, now a DSO cyborg, on bad terms.
- Is finally **cured of the body modification** by Kiryu, now on Gosha's staff.
- Nomad's agents (Oboro and the surgeon Fürst, disguised as academy medic) use Kosuke
  to implant a control device in her; it is removed, but Kosuke is taken and
  transformed.
- Prime Minister Asai sends her into a trap; the Taimanin are framed for an
  assassination and outlawed. While the Kanda Brigade occupies Gosha she spends days
  alone destroying the off-site servers that would expose Taimanin on undercover
  assignments — choosing the wider organisation over her own village.
- Shown that Sakura and Murasaki have been killed, she over-uses her demon form and is
  captured by Edwin Black.
- **Asagi route**: she kills demonised versions of her sister and friend, loses to
  Black, is fused into **Vampire Asagi**; the world resets.
- **Asuka route**: Asuka turns the tables, the two fight Black together; mid-fusion,
  Asagi drives a particle-charged kunai into Black's head, giving Asuka the opening to
  annihilate him. Asagi tells Asuka she is proud of her as a Taimanin.

## 5. Abilities

### 5.1 Ninja art — Falcon Art

Heightens senses, speed and physical ability; she moves about **six times** faster
than her already extreme baseline and perceives the world in slow motion. In-universe
researchers speculate that it manipulates Taima particles and the *flow of time* around
her rather than simply accelerating her. Visually: afterimages that read as multiple
Asagis.

**Cost and limit.** Extremely taxing; usable only in short bursts and not back-to-back.

| Technique | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Koujinka** (光陣華) | The slow-motion state itself: accelerated perception and thought; usable unarmed. |
| **Satsujinka** | A single fast, heavy strike; speed leaves a few duplicates. |
| **Satsujinki / "Blossom of Light"** | The finisher: five afterimage bodies deliver simultaneous slashes. At full power a threat even to noble-rank demons. |

### 5.2 General techniques

- **Mastery of fundamentals.** Her sword style is the standard Taimanin school and her
  weapon is the *trainee's* blade; she simply perfected both. This is a recurring
  point: she is not a specialist with a gimmick, she is the basics taken to the limit.
- **Hand-to-hand** — especially kicks ("Blade Legs"). **Hawk Slash** (horizontal cut),
  **Hirenka** (launch kick followed by four aerial slashes).
- **Speed and strength** — fastest Taimanin even without her art; it takes several orcs
  to hold her.
- **Particle control** — channels Taima particles into body and blade.
- **Inhuman Blood Art** — charges her own blood with spiritual energy so that it is
  poison to vampires; one of several anti-vampire measures she learned because she
  guessed Black's interest in her.
- **Counter-seduction technique** — uses spiritual energy to suppress arousal and the
  effects of body modification (taught by Natsu Inage); she is competent, not a master.
- **Vibration signalling** — covert communication at a distance (light novels).
- **Disguise and acting.**
- **Command** — strategist, political operator; can intimidate without fighting.

### 5.3 Inner demon — "Kage" / Demon Asagi

Awakened in *Asagi 2*. A controllable transformation that multiplies all her abilities,
lets her summon blades, and radiates a demonic aura. Appearance: violet skin, white
hair, black-and-yellow eyes, claws (in *Asagi 2*: white hair and a darker outfit).

Unlike most inner demons it behaves as a **second personality with hidden knowledge**:
it speaks to her in dreams, tempts her with victory, is manipulative, and regards human
and family ties with contempt. **The price:** if she ever gives in completely, the
Shadow takes the body permanently and Asagi's ego is destroyed. Overuse in one battle
is exactly how she loses in *Asagi 3*.

### 5.4 Vulnerabilities

- Falcon Art fatigue (burst only).
- The body modification (present in most timelines, cured only at the end of the VN
  line) — a permanent debuff she suppresses by willpower.
- Hostages: Sakura, Kosuke, Asuka, her students.
- Control arts that bypass physical strength (Oboro's puppeteering; soul-binding seals).
- The inner demon's corruption risk.
- Political constraints: she cannot strike targets the government protects.

## 7. Design reading — why the character works

Useful when designing an original mentor/commander NPC in the same genre.

1. **Peak of the basics.** Her power fantasy is mastery, not novelty: default weapon,
   default style, one simple art (speed) used perfectly. She is the benchmark every
   other character is measured against.
2. **A simple, legible art with a hard cost.** "Six times faster, in bursts, and it
   exhausts her" is a complete JRPG skill description: big effect, cooldown, self-cost.
3. **A second form with a real price.** The inner demon is a classic limit-break /
   berserk state whose risk is narrative (loss of self), not just a stat penalty.
4. **Strength that cannot solve the problem.** The stories beat her with hostages,
   politics, control effects and betrayal — never with a fair duel. Her plots are
   about will, not power.
5. **Three life stages = three roles.** Arrogant prodigy → grieving field captain →
   commander and teacher. By the online games she is the quest-giver who occasionally
   steps in and reminds everyone why she holds the title.
6. **Domestic contrast.** Housework, budgets, paperwork, a drink in the evening. The
   mundane detail is what makes the legend read as a person.
7. **A worn weakness.** A lasting injury from her first story that she manages daily
   by willpower — character and mechanic in one.

## 8. Conflicts between sources

- Her father's death: on duty `[VN]` vs murdered by his mother `[RPGX]`.
- Relationship with Kyousuke: central `[VN][AT]`, absent `[RPGX]`.
- Age at *Asagi 2*: 24 under the original one-year gap, 27 after the retcon.
- Whether she ever had an independent/rogue period: stated for her youth and for
  `[ZERO]`; `[VN]` only implies it.
- Demon form appearance differs between *Asagi 2* and later entries.
- Hair colour is given as dark blue or black depending on artist and entry.
